undergraduate

In spring 2024, graduated from UT Austin with a degree in neuroscience and minors in the social and behavioural sciences. At UT, I researched molecular and imaging biomarkers of MDD through NURP before joining Dr. Stephen Russell's lab to study how adverse childhood experiences affect sexual and gender minorities. Outside of research, I was also involved in many personal pursuits like outreach for asian american culture, oSTEM, TCCA, and atx scioly.

post-undergraduate

After graduation, I moved to Dallas to work in Dr. Wei Xu's lab at UTSW. There, I worked on the spatial mapping of neuronal activity in specific disease states. I then moved to Dr. Li Li's lab at TTUHSC, utilizing a multi-omics approach to explore the pharmacological basis behind the interaction of statins and Alzheimer's dementia. Outside of lab, I kept up with scioly and started volunteering at DARCC!
