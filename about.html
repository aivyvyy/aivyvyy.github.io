undergraduate me

In spring 2024, graduated from the University of Texas Austin with a major in neuroscience and minors in the social and behavioural sciences. During my time at UT, I started research on molecular and imaging biomarkers of MDD through NURP, and later the application of ACEs on the sexual-gender minorities in Dr. Stephen Russell's lab. I was also involved in many personal interests like outreach for asian american culture, oSTEM, TCCA, and atx scioly.

post-undergraduate me

After graduating, I moved out to Dallas to work in Dr. Wei Xu's lab at UTSW. There, I worked on the spatial mapping of neuronal activity in specific disease states. I then moved to Dr. Li Li's lab at TTUHSC, utilizing a multi-omics approach to understanding the pharmacological basis behind statins and Alzheimer's Dementia. I also continued to do scioly and began volunteering at DARCC!
